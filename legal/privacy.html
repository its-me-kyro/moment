Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Moment is a client-side web application. It does not require an account and does not send your data to any server operated by the developer.

Data we store

Settings (theme, timezone, font, colors, positions, custom background data URLs, message text, and similar preferences) are stored only in your browser’s localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export or share it yourself.

What we do not collect

  • No personal identification information
  • No analytics or tracking cookies from the app itself
  • No location data beyond the timezone you optionally select
  • No server-side logs of your usage

Third-party resources

Moment may load fonts from Google Fonts. Those requests are subject to Google’s own privacy policy. Custom background images you paste as remote URLs are fetched by your browser from the hosts you specify.

Your control

You can clear all Moment data at any time by using the Reset button in Settings or by clearing site data for this origin in your browser.
